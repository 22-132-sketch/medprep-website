# Owner checklist (off-page SEO, 5.8)

Rankings take weeks to months. Nothing here promises a result.

| # | Task | Where | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify `register.medprepapp.me` in Google Search Console (Domain property, DNS TXT record) | search.google.com/search-console | 10 min |
| 2 | Verify `medprepapp.me` (the app) as a second property | same | 10 min |
| 3 | Submit `https://register.medprepapp.me/sitemap.xml` in Search Console | Sitemaps | 2 min |
| 4 | Import both sites into Bing Webmaster Tools (one click from Search Console) | bing.com/webmasters | 5 min |
| 5 | After each new page: Search Console, URL Inspection, Request indexing | Search Console | 1 min |
| 6 | Test a share link in WhatsApp (send the homepage to yourself) and check the card shows the MedPREP image | WhatsApp | 2 min |
| 7 | Test the Open Graph card: paste a page URL into the Facebook Sharing Debugger and click Scrape Again | developers.facebook.com/tools/debug | 2 min |
| 8 | Check rich results on the home page | search.google.com/test/rich-results | 2 min |
| 9 | Keep YouTube, Instagram and Facebook bios linking to `register.medprepapp.me` (the site already lists them as `sameAs`) | each profile | 10 min |
| 10 | Create a Google Business Profile only if you have a public address or service area you want listed | business.google.com | optional |
| 11 | Share blog articles and the trial page in KMU student groups once articles exist | WhatsApp/Facebook groups | ongoing |
| 12 | Measure speed: paste each main URL into PageSpeed Insights (mobile), note LCP and CLS, repeat monthly | pagespeed.web.dev | 10 min |

Biggest long-term lever: marketing lives on a subdomain, so `medprepapp.me` shows only a login page to Google. Moving marketing to the root domain later would help most. You chose to keep both for now.
